Medical, Fitness & Nutrition Disclaimer

This Medical, Fitness & Nutrition Disclaimer outlines the clinical and legal boundaries of guidance provided through Dr Fit Veda. All users must read and acknowledge this notice prior to commencing programmes or scheduling consultations.
Instructions for Legal Counsel: Please confirm compliance of these disclaimers with the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines (2020) and statutory guidelines for AYUSH / Naturopathy / Dietetics professionals in India and cross-border telehealth advisory standards.

1. Supportive Wellness Care — Not Emergency or Hospital Care

Dr Fit Veda delivers online lifestyle modification, naturopathic consultation, nutritional guidance, and yoga/fitness training. Our services are strictly not an emergency medical service and do not replace comprehensive physical in-person clinical diagnosis, hospital admission, acute disease treatment, or direct surgical intervention by a primary healthcare physician.

Never ignore, disregard, or delay obtaining professional in-person medical advice because of recommendations, meal plans, or workout instructions received through this platform.

2. Emergency Medical Protocol (Immediate Action)

If you or someone under your care experiences severe or acute symptoms — including chest pain, crushing pressure, shortness of breath, sudden numbness, severe hemorrhage, loss of consciousness, stroke symptoms, allergic anaphylaxis, or acute psychiatric crisis — immediately contact emergency services:

India Emergency Ambulance: Dial 112 or 108
United Arab Emirates Emergency Services: Dial 998 (Ambulance) or 999 (Police)
Immediate Hospital Transfer: Transport the patient immediately to the nearest tertiary emergency department.

3. Pre-Existing Clinical Conditions and Medical Clearance

You are legally and medically obligated to consult your primary treating physician before commencing any programme if you:

  • Have diagnosed cardiovascular, renal, metabolic, endocrine, or pulmonary conditions (e.g., Type 1/Type 2 Diabetes, Hypertension, Coronary Artery Disease, Thyroid disorders, Chronic Kidney Disease, PCOD/PCOS);
  • Take daily prescription medications, including antihypertensives, insulin, oral hypoglycaemics, anticoagulants, or psychiatric medications;
  • Are pregnant, planning pregnancy, breastfeeding, recovering from recent surgery, or managing eating disorders;
  • Are over the age of 60 and initiating vigorous physical exercise for the first time.

Prescription Medication Inviolability: Never adjust, reduce, taper, or terminate prescription medications without the express written approval of your treating licensed medical doctor.

4. Physical Exercise and Yoga Safety

Physical exercise and yoga asanas carry inherent physiological risks of muscle strain, joint sprain, or cardiovascular stress. You must exercise in a hazard-free space, maintain adequate hydration, follow your trainer's instructions, and immediately cease any activity that produces acute pain, dizziness, or chest discomfort.

5. Nutritional Plans and Allergen Disclosures

Nutritional regimens are prepared based exclusively on user-provided dietary disclosures. It is your sole responsibility to review meal suggestions against known food allergies, histamine intolerances, or clinical dietary restrictions. Dietary supplements or herbal preparations must be verified for cross-interactions with prescription medications.

6. Non-Guarantee of Clinical Results and Weight Loss

Physiological outcomes vary substantially based on genetics, metabolic health, hormonal profiles, and adherence. Dr Fit Veda explicitly does not guarantee specific biological weight loss, chronic disease reversal, cure, or guaranteed clinical outcomes. Testimonials and success stories displayed on the platform reflect individual customer experiences and do not represent guaranteed outcomes.

7. Artificial Intelligence and Algorithmic Disclosures

The platform currently does not deploy autonomous generative artificial intelligence for clinical diagnosis or prescription generation. All care plans, diet charts, and assessments are reviewed and created by certified human healthcare and fitness professionals.

8. Professional Standards and Duty of Care

All contracted doctors, nutritionists, yoga therapists, and fitness coaches are credentialed professionals required to adhere to their respective statutory codes of ethics. Nothing in this disclaimer limits liability for medical negligence that cannot be excluded under applicable Indian law.
