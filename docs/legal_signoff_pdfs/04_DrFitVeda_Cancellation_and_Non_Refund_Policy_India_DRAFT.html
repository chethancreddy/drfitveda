Cancellation & Non-Refund Policy

This Cancellation & Non-Refund Policy sets forth the commercial terms and conditions governing digital membership subscriptions and standalone clinical/wellness consultations offered on drfitveda.com. All purchases are strictly non-refundable, as set forth herein, pursuant to the Indian Contract Act, 1872 and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.
Advocate Review Checklist (Indian Consumer Protection & E-Commerce Law): Please confirm that our strict non-refundable policy is legally compliant under Indian law when clearly and conspicuously disclosed prior to payment, and approve the removal/redefinition of any conflicting promotional claims (e.g., "100% Satisfaction Guarantee") to prevent exposure under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

1. Strictly Non-Refundable Commercial Policy

Dr Fit Veda delivers personalized lifestyle modification regimens, multi-disciplinary professional care (doctors, nutritionists, yoga therapists, fitness coaches), and dedicated consultation slots. Due to the customized allocation of professional time, proprietary diet plans, and infrastructure resources upon subscription:

MANDATORY NON-REFUNDABLE NOTICE: All subscription fees, membership packages, and consultation fees paid to Dr Fit Veda are strictly non-refundable and non-transferable under any circumstances once the transaction is completed. By making a payment, you expressly acknowledge and agree that no refunds, credits, or chargebacks will be issued for partially used, unused, or abandoned subscriptions.

2. Standalone Consultations: Rescheduling vs. Monetary Refund

  • Rescheduling Guidelines: Clients may reschedule an upcoming scheduled consultation (Yoga Doctor, PCOD Specialist, Wellness Doctor) without penalty by providing at least 24 hours' prior written notice via the client dashboard or email to care@drfitveda.com. The booking credit will be transferred to a mutually agreeable available slot.
  • Late Cancellations & No-Shows: Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice, or failure to attend a scheduled video session ("no-show"), will result in immediate forfeiture of the consultation session with zero monetary refund or credit.
  • Cancellations Initiated by Dr Fit Veda: In the rare event that an assigned healthcare professional is unavailable due to sudden illness, technical emergency, or outage, Dr Fit Veda will provide an expedited rescheduled slot at the client's earliest convenience.

3. Sole Technical Exceptions to Non-Refundable Rule

In compliance with statutory electronic transaction guidelines, monetary refunds are disbursed strictly and solely under the following verified technical anomalies:

  • Duplicate / Erroneous Billing: Where a verified technical glitch causes multiple identical charges for a single order transaction.
  • Payment Gateway Deduction without Service Creation: Where funds are debited from the client's bank account / card / UPI, but technical failure prevents user account creation or service activation on Dr Fit Veda.

In such instances, verified duplicate or un-activated debits will be reversed to the original payment instrument within 5 to 7 business days via our RBI-licensed payment gateway.

4. Mid-Term Discontinuation and Account Termination

Clients may discontinue participation in a wellness programme at any time. However, voluntary mid-term discontinuation, relocation, change of personal schedule, or unilateral cessation of adherence does not entitle the client to any pro-rata refund or monetary reimbursement for the remaining contract duration.

5. Promotional Claims and Satisfaction Warranties (Consumer Protection Act)

Dr Fit Veda delivers doctor-guided lifestyle guidance. Because biological outcomes, metabolic responses, and weight changes depend substantially on individual genetics, underlying pathologies, and daily adherence, Dr Fit Veda does not guarantee specific clinical or weight-loss results. Any promotional marketing reference to a "Satisfaction Guarantee" refers exclusively to our professional commitment to deliver scheduled consultations and customized regimens, and does not confer any contractual right to a monetary refund.
