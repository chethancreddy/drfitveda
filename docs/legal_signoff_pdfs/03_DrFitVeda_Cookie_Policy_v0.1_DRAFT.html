Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy details how Dr Fit Veda uses cookies and local browser storage technologies on drfitveda.com and its authenticated portals. It is based on a comprehensive audit of the active Next.js production codebase.
Instructions for Legal Counsel: Please confirm that our current deployment of strictly necessary cookies exempts the platform from displaying an intrusive cookie consent banner under Indian SPDI Rules, DPDP Act 2023, and international ePrivacy guidelines.

1. Summary and Zero-Tracking Commitment

Dr Fit Veda operates on a strict privacy-first foundation. The platform deploys only strictly necessary cookies required for user authentication, security verification, and role-based access control.

Zero Advertising and Analytics Trackers: We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, behavioural retargeting pixels (such as Meta Pixel or Google Ads), commercial tracking beacons, or cross-site fingerprinting scripts. Because strictly necessary cookies are exempt from prior consent requirements under ePrivacy guidelines and are not categorised as tracking under Indian or UAE data laws, no cookie consent banner is mandated at this time. Should non-essential analytics or marketing trackers be introduced in future releases, a compliant opt-in consent banner with granular controls will be deployed prior to firing any such scripts.

3. Self-Hosted Assets and Third-Party Isolation

To eliminate passive tracking and IP address leakage, all web typography (Nunito Sans) is self-hosted directly from our first-party origin (/fonts/). No connections are established with Google Fonts servers. All UI icons, sample video demonstrations, and SVG assets are served directly from our origin server.

When a customer joins an encrypted teleconsultation or live training session via Zoho Meeting, the connection is handed off to Zoho's video bridge, whereupon Zoho's independent security policies govern the real-time media transmission.

4. User Management of Browser Storage

Users can inspect, delete, or block cookies through standard browser settings. However, disabling strictly necessary cookies will inhibit user login and prevent access to the authenticated customer and professional dashboards.
