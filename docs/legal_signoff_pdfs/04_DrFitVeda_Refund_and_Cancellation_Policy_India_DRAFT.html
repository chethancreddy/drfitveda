Refund & Cancellation Policy

This Refund & Cancellation Policy sets forth the terms, timelines, and commercial conditions governing membership cancellations, consultation rescheduling, and financial reimbursements. It is drafted pursuant to the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

1. Scope of Purchased Services

  • Fixed-Term Wellness Memberships: Pre-paid subscription packages (1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, 12 Months) delivering customized fitness routines, clinical nutrition plans, yoga sessions, and multi-disciplinary professional supervision. Fixed-term subscriptions do not auto-debit.
  • Standalone Consultations: Per-session appointments with certified Doctors (Yoga Doctor, PCOD/PCOS Specialist, General Naturopathic Doctor) booked for designated slot durations.
  • Currency & Pricing: All transactions are conducted in Indian Rupees (₹ INR) inclusive of applicable taxes.

2. Cancellation Timelines and Guidelines

  • Membership Cancellations: Clients may initiate cancellation requests at any time by emailing care@drfitveda.com or via the client dashboard. Cancellation terminates upcoming scheduled sessions immediately.
  • Consultation Cancellations & Rescheduling: Standalone consultations may be rescheduled or cancelled up to [CONFIRM: 24 hours] prior to the appointed time without penalty, entitling the client to a full credit or refund. Cancellations made within less than 24 hours or no-shows are subject to [CONFIRM: 50% cancellation fee or slot forfeiture].
  • Cancellations by Dr Fit Veda: If a scheduled professional is unavailable due to medical emergency or technical outage, the client will be offered an immediate rescheduled slot or a 100% full refund for the affected session.

3. Statutory and Commercial Refund Eligibility

Clients are entitled to a refund under the following circumstances:

  • Billing Errors & Technical Double Charges: Where an erroneous duplicate debit occurs or payment succeeds without service activation.
  • Service Inability: Where Dr Fit Veda is unable to assign a qualified professional or permanently discontinues a booked programme.
  • Statutory Cooling-Off Window: Where cancellation is submitted within [CONFIRM: 7 calendar days] of initial purchase and the user has consumed no more than [CONFIRM: 2 sessions], refund is granted less the nominal fair value of delivered sessions.
  • Statutory Consumer Rights: Any non-waivable statutory refund rights guaranteed under the Indian Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020.
