Medical, Fitness & Nutrition Disclaimer

This Medical, Fitness & Nutrition Disclaimer sets forth the clinical boundaries, emergency exclusions, and risk warnings governing guidance provided through drfitveda.com. It is formulated in accordance with the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines, 2020 (issued by the National Medical Commission), professional codes of medical ethics, and the Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954.
Advocate Review Checklist (Medical & Advertising Compliance): Please review the disclaimer terms for compliance with Indian Telemedicine Guidelines and confirm that advertising copy conforms to the statutory prohibitions under the Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954.

1. Supportive Wellness Guidance — Strict Exclusion of Emergency Care

Dr Fit Veda provides doctor-guided naturopathy consultations, dietary lifestyle planning, yoga therapy, and fitness training delivered through electronic media. Services provided on this platform constitute supportive wellness and lifestyle guidance only.

Not an Emergency Medical Service: This platform is strictly not an emergency medical facility, critical care service, or hospital. It does not replace physical, in-person clinical diagnosis, clinical examination, pathology verification, or emergency hospital admission by a licensed primary care physician. Never ignore, disregard, or delay obtaining professional in-person medical advice because of recommendations or information received through this platform.

2. Emergency Medical Protocol (Immediate Action Required)

If you or someone under your care experiences acute, critical, or life-threatening symptoms — including crushing chest pain or pressure, severe shortness of breath, sudden numbness, loss of consciousness, uncontrolled hemorrhage, symptoms of stroke, acute allergic anaphylaxis, or psychiatric crisis — immediately contact emergency emergency services or proceed to the nearest hospital emergency room:

National Emergency Number (India): Dial 112
Ambulance Emergency Helpline (India): Dial 108 / 102
Immediate Hospital Transfer: Transport the patient immediately to the nearest tertiary hospital casualty / emergency department.

3. Pre-Existing Clinical Conditions and Treating Physician Clearance

Clients are legally and medically obligated to seek prior written clearance from their primary treating medical doctor before commencing any wellness or fitness regimen if they:

  • Have diagnosed cardiovascular, endocrine, renal, hepatic, or metabolic disorders (including Type 1/Type 2 Diabetes, Hypertension, Coronary Artery Disease, Thyroid disease, Chronic Kidney Disease, PCOD/PCOS);
  • Take daily prescription medications, including insulin, oral hypoglycaemics, antihypertensives, anticoagulants, or psychiatric medications;
  • Are pregnant, planning pregnancy, breastfeeding, recovering from surgical procedures, or managing eating disorders;
  • Are over the age of 60 and commencing physical training for the first time.

Prescription Medication Inviolability: Never adjust, taper, reduce, or stop prescription medications or clinical therapies without the express written direction of your treating licensed physician.

4. Physical Fitness and Yoga Asana Safety

Physical exercise, movement rehabilitation, and yoga asanas carry inherent physiological risks of muscle sprain, joint strain, ligament tear, or cardiovascular stress. Clients must exercise in a clear, hazard-free physical environment, maintain proper hydration, adhere strictly to trainer instructions, and immediately cease any activity that causes sharp pain, dizziness, or chest discomfort.

5. Nutritional Plans and Allergen Disclosures

Nutritional regimens are formulated based exclusively on client-provided dietary disclosures. Clients bear sole responsibility for verifying all meal recommendations against personal allergies, food intolerances, and clinical restrictions. Herbal or nutritional supplements must be cleared with treating doctors to avoid drug-nutrient cross-interactions.

6. Non-Guarantee of Biological Outcomes (DMR Act Compliance)

In compliance with the Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954 and ASCI Guidelines, Dr Fit Veda explicitly clarifies that physiological responses vary widely across individuals. Dr Fit Veda makes no claim, promise, or guarantee of complete disease cure, permanent reversal of chronic metabolic illnesses, or specific kilograms of weight loss. Testimonials on the website describe individual experiences and do not represent guaranteed or typical outcomes.

7. Artificial Intelligence and Professional Standards

The platform does not deploy autonomous generative artificial intelligence to issue medical diagnoses or generate prescriptions. All health consultations and regimens are evaluated and prepared by qualified human professionals (BNYS Naturopathic Doctors, certified nutritionists, certified yoga therapists, and fitness coaches). All professionals are required to maintain valid registrations and adhere to professional standards.
