Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy details the use of browser cookies and local storage technologies on drfitveda.com. It is based on a technical audit of the active Next.js production codebase and aligns with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
Advocate Review Checklist (Indian IT & Privacy Regulations): Please confirm our technical assessment that because the platform deploys strictly necessary first-party cookies and zero third-party advertising/tracking cookies, an intrusive consent popup banner is not mandated under Indian law.

1. Zero-Tracking Commitment and Architectural Summary

Dr Fit Veda operates on a strict privacy-first foundation. The platform deploys only strictly necessary, functional first-party cookies required to authenticate users, enforce role-based access control, and protect against cross-site request forgery.

Absence of Third-Party Advertising & Behavioural Pixels: We do not deploy third-party advertising networks, behavioural retargeting trackers (e.g., Meta Pixel, Google Ads remarketing), commercial beacons, or browser fingerprinting scripts. Because functional security cookies do not track user behaviour across external websites and are essential to deliver requested digital services, a separate cookie consent banner is not mandated under Indian privacy and IT regulations.

3. Self-Hosted Static Assets and Third-Party IP Isolation

To eliminate passive tracking and IP address leakage, all web typography (Nunito Sans) is self-hosted directly from our first-party origin (/fonts/). No connections are established with Google Fonts servers. All UI icons, sample video demonstrations, and SVG assets are served directly from our origin server.

When a customer joins an encrypted teleconsultation or live training session via Zoho Meeting, the connection is handed off to Zoho's video bridge, whereupon Zoho's independent security policies govern the real-time media transmission.

4. Browser Management and Deletion

Users can inspect, delete, or block cookies through browser preferences. However, disabling strictly necessary cookies will inhibit user login and prevent access to the authenticated customer and professional dashboards.
