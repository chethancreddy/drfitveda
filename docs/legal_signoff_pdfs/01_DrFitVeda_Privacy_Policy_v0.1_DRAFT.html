3. Legal Bases and Consent Mechanisms

India (DPDP Act 2023 & IT SPDI Rules 2011): We process personal data primarily on the basis of informed, explicit consent and for limited legitimate uses recognised by statute (e.g., fulfilling contracted services, complying with legal orders, or emergency medical response). Health information is classified as sensitive personal data. The platform enforces granular, separate, non-pre-ticked opt-in checkboxes prior to document uploads, consultation bookings, and session recordings. Acceptance of general Terms & Conditions is never conflated with consent for health data processing.

UAE and International Clients: For clients residing in the UAE or other jurisdictions, we rely on explicit consent for special categories of health data, performance of contract for wellness services, and legal compliance for transaction records. Consent may be revoked at any time; revocation does not invalidate prior lawful processing.

6. Technical and Organisational Security Measures

All communications are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Customer data is isolated at the database engine level using Row Level Security (RLS) policies. Diagnostic files and session recordings reside in private buckets accessed exclusively via temporary, cryptographically signed URLs. All administrative actions are recorded in immutable audit logs.

7. Minors and Eligibility

Services are restricted to individuals aged 18 years and older. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children under 18. Any account identified as belonging to an unauthorised minor will be terminated and associated data permanently expunged.

8. Data Subject Rights and Exercising Procedures

Under applicable data privacy legislation, users enjoy the following actionable rights:

  • Right of Access & Summary: Request a comprehensive export of all personal and health data stored on the platform.
  • Right to Correction & Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate, incomplete, or out-of-date personal information.
  • Right to Data Portability / Export: Receive personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable JSON/PDF format.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: Request deletion of personal data, subject to mandatory statutory retention exemptions.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke previously granted consents without retroactive penalty.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Submit privacy concerns directly to our appointed Grievance Officer.

Verification Procedure: To prevent identity theft and unauthorized disclosure, identity verification is conducted via existing verified credentials before releasing or deleting any data.

Statutory Deletion Exemptions: Where clinical, accounting, tax, or fraud prevention laws mandate record preservation, certain records cannot be immediately deleted. In such instances, records are segregated, access-restricted, and retained solely for statutory defense.

9. Mandatory Record Retention Policies

Medical guidelines, accounting statutes, and electronic transaction laws impose strict retention periods. Consultation notes and medical histories are preserved in accordance with Indian medical regulatory norms [CONFIRM: 3 years under Telemedicine Practice Guidelines / NMC regulations] before automated anonymisation or deletion.
